`D3.js` `JavaScript` `web cartography` &nbsp;·&nbsp; Mar.–May 2018

Undergraduate thesis, collaboration with SinoMaps Press.
Advisors: **Prof. Haihong Zhu** and **Yuntao Long**.

A survey of web-based thematic-mapping methods, followed by an architecture —
overall, functional, and logical — for online visualization, interaction, and
dynamic data updates.

The prototype is a D3.js customs trade map with a map-service layer, supporting
thematic visualization, statistical charts, interactive queries, and live data
updates.
