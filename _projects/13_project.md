`Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB Atlas` `Pug` `Google OAuth 2.0` &nbsp;·&nbsp; Nov. 2023

A full-stack review application: browse all reviews or a single one, and write
or modify your own.

Node.js and Express with Mongoose against MongoDB Atlas, server-rendered with
Pug. Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication. Deployed to Google Cloud.
