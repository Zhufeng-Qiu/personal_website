`React` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB Atlas` `Google OAuth 2.0` &nbsp;·&nbsp; Nov.–Dec. 2023

A MERN application for previewing active teams and players, comparing them, and
creating or updating a fantasy roster.

Node.js and Express on the back end with Mongoose against MongoDB Atlas; React
on the front. Google OAuth 2.0 handles login and logout. Deployed to Google
Cloud. I split the project, assigned tasks, and tracked progress across the
team.
