Capacity ≠ speed.

A LoRA-tuned Llama 3.1 8B price estimator: 175 prompt tokens in, 5 tokens out. 4-bit weights are what let it fit on a 16 GB T4, yet on an A10 they make every decode step slower. Step through one request and watch where the milliseconds go.

Same A10 · unquantized fp16 vs NF4

4-bit buys capacity, not speed.

NF4 streams far fewer weight bytes per decode step and still takes longer: it is bound by dequantization, not by bandwidth.

The byte ratio is weights-only arithmetic (fp16 16.06 GB against NF4 with double quantization at 4.14 GB); decode time and bandwidth utilization are measured. On a 16 GB T4, fp16 does not fit at all: 4-bit is the precondition for running, not a tuning knob.
