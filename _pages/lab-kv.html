Fewer bytes ≠ less time.

Qwen3-1.7B's KV cache, 234.9 MB, compressed 3.02× by an error-bounded codec. Between two GPUs that is a loss; on a durable write it is a win. Same cache, same codec: the path sets the sign.

Same cache, same 3.02× fewer bytes, opposite signs. Between GPUs, encoding and decoding cost more than the smaller transfer saves; on the durable write, the saved bytes win.

Two more measured paths: host-staged serial GPU → GPU, 27.87 against 53.35 ms (1.91× slower; paired R 3.14); overlay fsync write, 130.0 against 62.9 ms (2.07× faster; one run, not in the paired round).

The path sets the sign.

360 raw/compressed pairs taken back to back, half in each order, across three segments of one session on 2× A40. Four caches, three paths. The magnitudes drift; the direction never does.

measured

R = geometric mean of Tcompressed / Traw over 30 pairs, with its 95% interval, on a log scale; the vertical line is R = 1. The fsync path here is MooseFS over the data-centre network. The wide serial interval (d01 · 1024) is reported as measured; slow samples were not removed.

02 Why host-staged? cudaMemcpyPeer returned success and moved nothing, on 3 of 3 rented multi-GPU pods.

Observed on 3 of 3 rented multi-GPU pods

A success code that moved nothing.

can_device_access_peer said yes. cudaMemcpyPeer returned cudaSuccess. The destination stayed zero, both directions, 4 B through 32 MB, ten of ten attempts. Through host memory the copy was exact.

Two GPU models (A40, RTX A6000), two data centres, PXB and SYS topologies. Characterised, not diagnosed: the cause and how common it is both need host access a rented pod does not give. Every transport number above is host-staged for this reason.

Source: BoundRelay research note §2 (“Where compression pays”), §3 and §4, and the paired protocol round of 2026-09-17. Qwen3-1.7B at revision b9352fbb, bf16; one 234.9 MB cache; 2× A40 and 2× A6000 rented pods. Raw arms verified byte for byte; compressed arms held 0.9999 × ε.

The single-run times compare arms measured at different moments on a shared machine; the paired round fixes the sign. The fsync figures are acknowledged writes, not round trips. One model, one corpus, one GPU generation: the break-even (3.82 → 5.38 GB/s) is implied by measured costs, not measured.
