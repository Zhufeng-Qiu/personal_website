How is it lossless? · The mechanism

Six integers. Two words.

Per item pair the kernel accumulates (n, Σx, Σy, Σx², Σy², Σxy) over co-raters. Ratings are 1–5 stars, so all six are small exact integers: their dataset-bound widths add up to 85 bits, carried in 384 bits of float64. Two GPUs sum partial statistics for 1,171,857 item pairs.

How the models work

With baseline time = 1, communication share s, and compression ratio r (1 or 3), synchronous time is (1 − s) + s/r. An encode + decode cost h adds to it, so compression pays while h < s(1 − 1/r): the break-even marked on the slider.

No collective launch latency, contention, chunking, or bandwidth changes are modeled. That is why the formula does not reproduce the recorded NVLink and PCIe results, and why the overlap chapter shows measurements rather than a model.

Recorded snapshot: the three-regime table in analysis.md (September 2026 measurement audit). Communication shares 11.6 / 76.0 / 91.1%; packed time reductions 4.7 / 23.5 / 55.5%; async time changes +19.3 / +9.7 / −4.3%. Figures belong to that table, not the later README headline results. Hosts used comparable builds, not a verified identical binary, and PCIe figures predate warp packing. These are configurations, not a controlled interconnect-only comparison.

The PCIe SYS +9.7% result predates moving finalize off the communication stream. Later traces identify that scheduling choice as important, and later runs do not support a universal “async loses” conclusion. Read the current claim ledger for the revised scope.

Payload is the AllReduce input representation: 48 → 16 bytes per item pair (56.2 → 18.7 MB across item_full), not a measured network-wire byte counter. The packing demo uses simulated ratings with the real layout and dataset bounds from the Pearson contract §§9–10; this browser packs and adds with BigInt, not on a GPU. The documented fixture result max_abs_diff = 0.0 is a numerical-equality check, not a bit-for-bit comparison of floating-point output.
